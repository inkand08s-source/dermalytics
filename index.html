Analysis Limitations & Forensic Constraints (Click to Expand) +

This prototype does not perform molecular assay or depth tomography. The user must account for the following established scientific limitations:

1. Lighting & White Balance Ambient chromatic temperature and specular reflections alter digital RGB channels without reflectance standards.
2. Epidermal Melanin Baseline Constitutive skin pigmentation absorbs and scatters visible light, attenuating underlying contusion chromophores differently across Fitzpatrick types.
3. Anatomical & Depth Variation Microvascular architecture, subcutaneous adipose thickness, and tissue pressure dictate extravasation depth and observable surface migration rates.
4. Individual Healing Kinetics Metabolic rate, age, vascular integrity, and pharmacological anti-coagulation substantially shift macroscopic resolution timelines.
5. Lack of Longitudinal Dataset Current logic is heuristic and rule-based; it lacks validation on statistically significant, calibrated multi-temporal clinical cohorts.
6. Camera Hardware Divergence Different sensor CMOS Bayer filters, compression artifacts (JPEG), and proprietary image signal processors (ISP) skew linear color values.
